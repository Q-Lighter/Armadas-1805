What is stored

Your username, your display name, and a password that Supabase stores hashed. We never see or store your password in readable form.

Later game stats and saves may also be stored with your account.

No email

There is no email address for players. Supabase receives a made-up address built from your username so its login system can work. No email is sent to it.

Deleting your account

Use Delete account in the Account screen. It removes your profile and your Supabase login. If you are in a running game, deleting your account counts as resigning from that game.
